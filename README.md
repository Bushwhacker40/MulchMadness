#Mulch Madness - 
##A Full Stack fundraiser management and delivery platform built for a Boy Scouts troop.

**Screenshot Pending**

###Status - Under Development.

###<u>Why Mulch Madness?</u>
Mulch Madness was created to assist a local Boy Scout troop.  It is designed to assist with the troop's main fundraiser to fund scout events and campouts.  The fundraiser consists of scouts selling mulch to local homes in   Iowa and the customers are given the option to have the mulch spread by the scouts for an additional cost.  Sales average around 10,000 bags and are either delivered to the customer's home or spread all in one weekend.      This undertaking takes an immense amount of coordination on both scouts and parents.  Prior to Mulch Madness, this process was handled with pen and paper and a lot of organizing by the main coordinator.  Mulch Madness       will step in to help scouts submit orders and organize deliveries/spreads.

###<u>Key Features</u>
- Role-based accounts for Admins, Scouts and Parents.
- Digital sales capture and reporting.
- Delivery/Spread optimization with Mapbox API.

###<u>Tech Stack</u>
Frontend: React, TypeScript, Vite, Bootstrap 5, Sass
Backend: NestJS, PostgreSQL
Other: Mapbox
